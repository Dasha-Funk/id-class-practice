Ordered and Unordered Lists

LISTS

Ordered List

Ordered lists have numbers to them. Great for creating a list to do things in a certain order.

  1. Coffee
  2. Donuts
  3. Cake
  4. Ice cream
  5. Cream Cheese
  6. Pasta
  7. Spagethhi Sauce
  8. Candy
  9. Books
  10. Tea
  11. Honey

Unordered List

Unordered Lists have bullets. Great for a grocery lists. Not having to complete things in order.

  • pajamas
  • shampoo
  • clothes
  • tent
  • food
  • flashlight
  • shovel
  • wood
  • water

Descriptive List

Descriptive Lists are usually reserved for things such as glossaries. DL is the tag used to define descriptive list. However, like ul uses li inside of it, dl uses dt and dd inside of it. Dl is the list of terms whereas Dt is the term and dd is the description of the term. Example below.

Coffee (the term)
Hot, Black drink (the description of the term)
Milk
White, cold drink
